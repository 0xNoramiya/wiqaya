1. What Wiqaya is

Wiqaya is a free, open-source browser extension that monitors time spent on websites you choose to track and overlays a Quran verse when configured time limits are reached. It was created as a submission for the Quran Foundation Hackathon 2026 and is provided as a personal-use tool, not a commercial product.

2. Quran content and the Quran Foundation

All Quran text, translations, and audio recitations displayed by Wiqaya are retrieved from the Quran Foundation APIs at the time of use. Wiqaya does not redistribute Quran content; it acts as a client of those APIs. Use of those APIs is also governed by any applicable terms set by the Quran Foundation. Please respect the source materials and the reciters whose voices are made available through those APIs.
